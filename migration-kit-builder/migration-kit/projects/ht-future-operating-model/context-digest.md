# Context digest — HT Future Operating Model

## Purpose

Define HT's strategic operating-model trajectory through 2030 and 2040 —
board-ready frameworks that are analytically rigorous and visually
compelling for executives. Constraints: Deutsche Telekom Group context,
selective HT autonomy, full network owner-operator stance, Pluto as the
data platform underpinning data-as-a-product (including external
monetization).

## Three core deliverables already produced

1. **Future Operating Model for 2030+ — "lights-out telco" archetype.**
   Full infrastructure ownership combined with radical automation.
   Six-layer structure:
   - North Star constraints
   - Strategic Design Principles
   - Capability Architecture (12 clusters across 3 tiers)
   - Operating Model Blueprint (6 dimensions)
   - Transition Architecture in three horizons: 2025–2027 Foundation,
     2027–2029 Scale, 2029–2031 Complete
   - Governance & Steering

   Confirmed parameters: revenue dominated by core telco (<10% beyond-
   core), 4 firm digital targets (95% digital channels, 100% AI L1
   support, zero-touch operations, fully digital products), 55/45
   B2C/B2B with enterprise as growth lever, **workforce reduction
   from ~5,500 to minimum ~4,125 FTEs**, IT dissolved into business
   domains.

2. **2040 Vision — "segment-sovereign telco."** Fundamental shift from
   domain-organized to **segment-organized** around 7 customer segments
   (Family, Smart Shopper, Digital Basic, Young, Modern Loyalist, SMB,
   Enterprise). Includes segment team compositions blending human roles
   and AI agents, a 12-skill framework, 8 central platform functions,
   and a people model projecting **~2,800 FTEs by 2040**.

   **Critical sequencing principle:** the 2030 domain model is a
   *precondition* for 2040 segment sovereignty, not merely a waypoint.
   Conflating the two phases would undermine both.

3. **45 structured assumptions** underpinning the 2030 TOM across 8
   categories (market, customer, technology, AI/automation, network,
   product, financial, workforce, regulatory). **Four highest-risk
   assumptions flagged:**
   - **A16** — BSS modernization without full rip-and-replace.
     If false, the €150–250M investment envelope explodes and the
     timeline shifts 2–3 years.
   - **A22** — Scaling AI & Data Tribe from ~25 to 80–100 by 2028.
     The capacity constraint everything else depends on.
   - **A38** — Engineering talent supply in Croatia (4M-person market).
     Structurally scarce.
   - **A19** — 100% AI L1 support including the long-tail 200+ low-
     frequency edge cases (not just the top 10 contact reasons).

## Way of Work — recently anchored

A WoW principles framework is now in scope as the fourth deliverable.
Three foundational choices made:

- **Work rhythm: hybrid — continuous for business, sprint-based for
  engineering.** Deliberately inverted from the typical tech-company
  pattern. Logic: market signals don't wait for sprint boundaries
  (continuous business), but telco engineering with 200+ integrations
  needs structured (short, weekly) cadences for stability.
- **Human ↔ AI relationship: context-dependent** (driven by task type
  and risk).
- **Decision-making: empowered teams with guardrails.**

Sequencing principle established: WoW *principles* come first → drive
the operating model design → operating model then drives the WoW
detailed design (rituals, tools, decision processes). 8 WoW principles
were drafted; W5 ("Gradimo, ne naručujemo" — we build, not buy) is
flagged as the principle most likely to face organizational resistance.
The **A1 competitive-response scenario** (53 hours vs 6 weeks for the
same decision) is the highlighted artefact for executive socialisation.

skills gap, low automation. Operational flow templates and pillar-
