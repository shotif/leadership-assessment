# Context digest — PetAIk

## Purpose

**PetAIk** = "petak" (Friday in Croatian) + AI. A monthly, full-day
AI-upskilling-and-production format for HT employees, framed as the
"production day of the future" — not a free day, not a training day.
Single mechanism designed to deliver three goals at once: upskilling,
productivity, and culture change.

## Key decisions taken so far

- **HT-own, not via DT group programs.** Rationale: HT is faster and
  more advanced; speed beats coordination. This is the framing for
  the inevitable Board question.
- **Cadence: 1 day per month** for the pilot (instead of every Friday).
  Each PetAIk becomes an "event" with weight rather than a routine that
  gets diluted.
- **Pilot timing: Q2 2026.**
- **Day shape: 8h, 5 blocks** — Arrival (15m) → Spark (75m) → Build I
  (120m) → lunch → Build II (90m) → Show (60m) → Reflect (30m).
  Build is the day's centre of gravity (~3.5h of deep work).
- **Three audience profiles, one brand:**
  - **A — Builders** (AI & Data Tribe, IT, devs): technical challenge,
    sandbox, peer code/prompt review.
  - **B — Adopters** (marketing, sales, finance, HR, legal, C-suite):
    no/low-code, prompt engineering, automating own workflows.
  - **C — Front** (retail, call centre, field): "Floating PetAIk" —
    the right to one AI day inside the calendar week, agreed with shift
    lead. Mobile-first, ~2h, content tuned to front-relevant tools.
    Solves the "we can't take Friday off" objection that kills these
    initiatives elsewhere.
- **AI Champion network** as distributed facilitators. Peer, not
  expert — "feature, not bug."
- **Two challenges defined for the first pilot:**
  - PetAIk #1 — individual: "Moj posao, ali s AI-em" (each participant
    automates their own pain point).
  - PetAIk #2 — team: mixed A+B teams on a defined use case.
- **Measurement without ROI in the early phase.** Output count,
  adoption rate, self-reported time saved, NPS, plus qualitative
  material for the eventual Board story.
- **Pilot scope: ~30–50 people.** AI & Data Tribe + one business unit
  (Sales/Marketing — leveraging momentum from Vibe Code Face-Off).
